Five DevZ mods in one download: bigger inventories, bigger ammo stacks, longer-lasting guns, kits that combine, and repairs back to Pristine.

[h2]What's inside[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Inventory Sizes[/b]: more room in clothing, backpacks, vests and pouches.
[*] [b]Ammo Stacks[/b]: loose ammo stacks twice as big.
[*] [b]Weapon & Suppressor Durability[/b]: weapons and suppressors last 10x as long.
[*] [b]Combine Kits[/b]: combine part-used bandages, repair kits and filters, but never split them.
[*] [b]Repair To Pristine[/b]: repair kits repair items all the way to Pristine instead of stopping at Worn.
[/list]
Each one is also on the Workshop on its own. [b]Use either this bundle or the single mods, never both[/b]: they're the same files, so loading both loads every change twice.

[h2]Default settings[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Inventory Sizes:[/b] Most items get a little less than double their vanilla space (typically 50-90% more). The largest backpacks go further (Field, Long Haul, Tactical, Mountain, up to 10x16), so they carry sheet metal, barrels and vehicle parts.
[*] [b]Ammo Stacks:[/b] 2x stacks: 9x19 goes from 25 to 50, 12ga from 10 to 20, .22 from 50 to 100. Single-round items (rockets, 40mm grenades, darts) stay at 1. Loot: run [b]fix_loot.py[/b] from the GitHub repo once on your types.xml so the ammo found in the world stays the same size. Skip it if you want more ammo in the world.
[*] [b]Weapon & Suppressor Durability:[/b] Weapons last 10x as long. Suppressors and other muzzle attachments last 10x as long.
[*] [b]Combine Kits:[/b] Bandage, Duct Tape, Electrical Repair Kit, Epoxy Putty, Gas Mask Filter, Improvised Gas Mask Filter, Leather Sewing Kit, Sewing Kit, Tire Repair Kit, Gun Cleaning Kit, Sharpening Stone.
[*] [b]Repair To Pristine:[/b] Every vanilla kit: Gun Cleaning Kit, Sewing Kit, Leather Sewing Kit, Duct Tape, Sharpening Stone, Epoxy Putty, Electrical Repair Kit, Tire Repair Kit, Blowtorch. Kits built on these count too.
[/list]
This Workshop build covers [b]vanilla items[/b], so it works on any server and needs no other mod.

[h2]Server setup[/h2]
[list]
[*] Add it to [b]-mod=[/b] (players need it too; they download it automatically when they join).
[*] Copy [b]keys\DevZ.bikey[/b] from the mod folder into your server's [b]keys[/b] folder.
[*] Ammo Stacks: run [b]fix_loot.py[/b] from the GitHub repo once on your types.xml so the ammo found in the world stays the same size.
[/list]

[h2]Your own settings and modded items[/h2]
Every mod in the bundle comes with a [b]scanner[/b] that reads your server and every mod you run (clothing, weapons, kits...) and lists their items in a simple table, next to the vanilla ones. Change any values, build, and you have copies made for your exact mod list.
[list]
[*] The settings are built into the mods, so this download only holds the finished mods. The tables, scanners and build scripts are on [url=https://github.com/DevilinJake/DevZ-Mods]GitHub[/url].
[*] Step-by-step guide, no programming needed: [url=https://github.com/DevilinJake/DevZ-Mods/blob/main/CUSTOMISING.md]CUSTOMISING.md[/url]
[*] Want only some of them, or your own settings for one? Load the single mods (or your own copies) instead of this bundle.
[/list]
